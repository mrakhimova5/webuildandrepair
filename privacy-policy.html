Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here's how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when you request an estimate, schedule a consultation, or contact us. This includes your name, contact details, property address, and project specifications.

How We Use Your Information

Your information helps us to:

  • Prepare accurate project estimates
  • Schedule and coordinate construction services
  • Communicate project updates and timelines
  • Follow up on completed projects for warranty purposes
  • Improve our services based on feedback

Information Protection

We never sell your personal information. We only share data with subcontractors and suppliers as needed to complete your project, and they are required to maintain confidentiality.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information. Project documents and contracts are stored securely and accessible only to authorized team members.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us.
